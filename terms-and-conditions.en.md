### 5.2. Three-Stage Quota and Credit Consumption Waterfall

5.2.1. Metered AI Agent actions consist of AI chat responses and Virtual Try-On image generations. Each metered action consumes quota or Credit atomically in the following strict three-stage order:

5.2.2. Stage 1 (Monthly Package Quota): The Platform first deducts one (1) unit multiplied by the selected AI Model's Quota Multiplier from the Customer's remaining Monthly Package Quota for chat responses or Virtual Try-On generations.

5.2.3. Stage 2 (Monthly Credit Pool): Once the applicable Monthly Package Quota is exhausted (or does not contain enough units to cover the AI Model's Quota Multiplier), the Platform deducts the action's applicable Credit tariff from the Customer's remaining Monthly Credit Pool for the current Billing Cycle.

5.2.4. Stage 3 (Credit Balance): Once both Stage 1 and Stage 2 are exhausted, the Platform deducts the action's applicable Credit tariff from the Customer's prepaid Credit Balance, unless the Customer has enabled the setting to block Credit Balance overage consumption.

5.2.5. Fail-Closed Rejection: If all enabled stages have insufficient quota or Credit to cover the requested action, the Platform rejects the action without executing model inference. Exhaustion of quotas or Credits stops metered AI responses until quota or Credit is replenished, but does not suspend the Subscription Plan or delete Customer Content.

5.2.6. Unused Monthly Package Quota units and unused Monthly Credit Pool allowances expire at the end of each Billing Cycle and do not roll over to subsequent Billing Cycles.

### 5.3. AI Model Tiers, Multipliers, and Per-Action Credit Tariffs

5.3.1. Each Subscription Plan specifies the AI Models available to the Customer. Standard AI Models operate with a default Quota Multiplier of one (1), while higher-tier AI Models may carry a Quota Multiplier greater than one (1) as displayed in the Main Dashboard.

5.3.2. When metered actions are charged against the Monthly Credit Pool (Stage 2) or Credit Balance (Stage 3), the Platform deducts the per-action Credit tariff configured for the selected AI Model and action type (which defaults to two hundred fifty (250) Credits per chat response and two thousand (2,000) Credits per Virtual Try-On generation unless a specific model tariff is displayed in the Main Dashboard).

### 5.4. Credit Balance Top-Ups, Overage Controls, and Usage Reversals

5.4.1. Customers with an active Subscription Plan may purchase prepaid Credit Balance top-ups in Indonesian Rupiah at a conversion rate of one (1) Credit per one (1) Indonesian Rupiah (1 Credit = 1 IDR), subject to a minimum top-up of ten thousand (10,000) Credits/IDR and a maximum top-up of one hundred million (100,000,000) Credits/IDR per transaction, and a maximum of three (3) concurrent pending top-up invoices.

5.4.2. Settled Credit Balance top-ups are recorded in an append-only double-entry ledger and do not expire at the end of a Billing Cycle.

5.4.3. The Customer may at any time toggle the Credit Balance overage setting in the Main Dashboard to either permit or prohibit Stage 3 deductions from its Credit Balance.

5.4.4. If a metered AI chat response or Virtual Try-On generation fails due to an internal execution error before completion and a usage reversal is triggered by the AI Agent runtime, the Platform restores the exact Monthly Package Quota units, Monthly Credit Pool amount, or Credit Balance amount deducted for that failed action once, protected by idempotency controls.

### 5.5. Knowledge Base Document Slot Limits and Downgrade Gates

5.5.1. Each Subscription Plan enforces an aggregate limit on the number of Knowledge Base documents stored across all of the Customer's AI Agents. Uploading a document claims one slot, and deleting a document releases one slot.

5.5.2. The Platform enforces a downgrade gate that blocks switching to or activating a Subscription Plan whose Knowledge Base document limit is lower than the Customer's currently stored document count until the Customer deletes sufficient documents to bring its usage within the target plan's limit.

### 5.6. Plan Switching and Early Renewal Prepayment

5.6.1. An active Customer may request a change of Subscription Plan or Billing Cycle during a running period. Whether paid immediately as a new plan prepayment or scheduled for the next renewal invoice, the new Subscription Plan's quotas, feature limits, and Billing Cycle take effect strictly at the transition to the next Billing Cycle and never mid-period.

5.6.2. An active Customer may prepay the single immediately following Billing Cycle prior to automatic invoice generation. For annual Subscription Plans, early renewal prepayment opens one (1) calendar month before the end of the current annual period. Once the next period is prepaid, plan switching is locked until that prepaid period becomes active.

5.6.3. If a newly applied Subscription Plan at period transition has a lower maximum active AI Agent limit than the number of AI Agents currently running, or does not include a Third-Party Channel previously enabled by the Customer, the Platform automatically stops excess live AI Agents into a pending state (subject to the fifteen-day Retention Window) and disables the unsupported Third-Party Channel connections.

### 7.1. Overdue Renewal Invoices and the Three-Day Grace Period

7.1.1. If a renewal invoice remains unpaid after its due date at the end of the Billing Cycle, the Customer's subscription automatically enters the Grace Period for three (3) calendar days ([GRACE PERIOD DAYS]).

7.1.2. ### 7.2. Billing Suspension and Stoppage of Live AI Agents

7.2.1. If the overdue renewal invoice remains unpaid upon expiration of the Grace Period (and no invoice for the subscription is held in the manual review state), the subscription automatically transitions to Billing Suspension.

7.2.2. Upon entering Billing Suspension, the Platform immediately stops all live AI Agents belonging to the Customer, terminates their active runtime containers, and removes public subdomain routing so that the Hosted Web Storefront, Web Widget, WhatsApp, and Telegram channels cease responding.

### 7.3. Fifteen-Day Post-Suspension Data Retention and Reactivation

7.3.1. Upon entering Billing Suspension, the Platform initiates the fifteen (15) calendar day Retention Window ([SUSPENSION RETENTION DAYS]), during which each stopped AI Agent's dedicated database (including Product Catalog items, Knowledge Base documents, Virtual Try-On bindings, AI Instructions, Operator Accounts, and branding) is preserved intact.

7.3.2. If the Customer settles the overdue renewal invoice during the Grace Period or before the expiration of the fifteen-day Retention Window, the Platform reactivates the Subscription Plan for the new Billing Cycle and automatically restores and resumes the AI Agents that were stopped due to Billing Suspension.

### 7.4. Voluntary Cancellation at Period End Without Grace Period

7.4.1. An active Customer may submit a voluntary subscription cancellation request from the Main Dashboard by verifying their account password, providing a cancellation reason, and entering the required confirmation phrase.

7.4.2. Upon submitting a voluntary cancellation request, the subscription is marked to cancel at the end of the current Billing Cycle. The Customer's AI Agents remain active until the end of the current Billing Cycle, no renewal invoice is generated, and the Customer may revoke the cancellation request and resume normal renewal at any time prior to the end of the current Billing Cycle.

7.4.3. If the voluntary cancellation is not revoked prior to the end of the current Billing Cycle, then immediately upon the end of that Billing Cycle the subscription bypasses the Grace Period, transitions directly to Billing Suspension, stops all live AI Agents, and begins the fifteen (15) calendar day Retention Window.

### 7.5. Permanent Deletion of AI Agent Data and Preserved Account Records

7.5.1. If a subscription remains in Billing Suspension past the expiration of the fifteen (15) calendar day Retention Window (verified with a five-minute recent-payment safety guard and absence of any invoice under manual review), the subscription transitions to terminated status.

7.5.2. Upon termination after the Retention Window, the Platform permanently and irreversibly drops each AI Agent's dedicated database, resets Knowledge Base document slot counters, purges raw Visitor Analytics session reports and per-visitor records, deletes the Customer's AI Agent builder configurations, and marks any remaining unpaid invoices as expired.

7.5.3. Termination of a subscription and deletion of AI Agent data does not delete the Customer's Main Dashboard user account, remaining prepaid Credit Balance and ledger history, or historical invoice and payment records, enabling the Customer to sign in and purchase a new Subscription Plan in the future.

### 7.6. Per-Agent Data Retention Following Plan Downgrade or Administrative Stop

7.6.1. 7.6.2. If that individual AI Agent is not resumed within the fifteen-day Retention Window, the Platform permanently drops that AI Agent's dedicated database and raw Visitor Analytics records while preserving the non-deployed service configuration row in the Main Dashboard.
