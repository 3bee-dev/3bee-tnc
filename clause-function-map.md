## 1. Legend of Required Legal Functions (Functions 1 to 6)

Every article and annex in [docs/platform-legal/terms-and-conditions.id.md](./terms-and-conditions.id.md) and [docs/platform-legal/terms-and-conditions.en.md](./terms-and-conditions.en.md) is engineered and tagged to serve one or more of the six required legal functions:

- **Function 1 (F1 — Binding Contract)**: Establishes a legally binding contract between the Provider and the Customer (capacity, offer, electronic acceptance, consideration, term, amendments, governing law).
- **Function 2 (F2 — Rights and Obligations)**: Clearly states the operational, commercial, billing, data-processing, and support rights and obligations of both the Provider and the Customer.
- **Function 3 (F3 — Misuse Risk Mitigation)**: Mitigates operational, regulatory, AI-safety, channel, and security risks against misuse or abuse of the Platform by Customers, Operator Accounts, or End Users.
- **Function 4 (F4 — Reasonable Limitation of Liability)**: Allocates commercial risk through balanced disclaimers, probabilistic AI disclosures, third-party channel dependencies, indemnification, and a proportional liability cap with mandatory-law and gross-negligence/willful-misconduct exceptions.
- **Function 5 (F5 — Intellectual Property Protection)**: Protects the Provider's proprietary Platform software, templates, guardrails, and brand, while clarifying Customer Content ownership, limited licenses, AI Output rights, and subdomain/trademark rules.
- **Function 6 (F6 — Usable Evidence in a Dispute)**: Establishes the admissibility and prima facie evidentiary weight of electronic acceptance logs, OTP records, double-entry wallet ledgers, usage metering logs, payment webhook signature verifications, and audit logs.
