### 26.1. Applicability, Role of the Merchant Customer, and Role of the Platform

26.1.1. These Consumer-Facing Terms ([Annex F, located at annexes/annex-f-consumer-facing-terms.en.md](./annex-f-consumer-facing-terms.en.md)) apply to every End User, shopper, or visitor who interacts with an AI Agent, Hosted Web Storefront, Web Widget, WhatsApp account, or Telegram bot powered by the 3Bee Platform.

26.1.2. The store, brand, products, and services presented by the AI Agent are owned, operated, and sold independently by the respective merchant business (Merchant) named on the storefront or messaging channel.

26.1.3. [COMPANY LEGAL NAME] (3Bee) is solely the technology provider supplying the software infrastructure for the AI Agent under the [Service Agreement (terms-and-conditions.en.md)](../terms-and-conditions.en.md). 3Bee does not own, manufacture, stock, price, sell, ship, or warrant any product or service offered by the Merchant, and 3Bee is not a party to any purchase, booking, or payment transaction between the End User and the Merchant.

### 26.2. Automated AI Responses, Product Information, and Verification by End Users

26.2.1. When an End User chats with the AI Agent, responses are generated automatically by artificial intelligence software based on product catalogs and instructions configured by the Merchant.

26.2.2. Because artificial intelligence responses are generated probabilistically, replies regarding product specifications, availability, prices, discounts, shipping terms, or store policies may occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate. End Users must confirm final prices, stock availability, product suitability, and order terms directly with the Merchant before completing any purchase or payment.

26.2.3. The AI Agent is an automated shopping and customer-service assistant and does not provide medical, legal, financial, or safety advice.

### 26.3. Virtual Try-On Preview Terms, Photo Uploads, and Mandatory Disclaimer

26.3.1. Where the Merchant enables the Virtual Try-On feature, an End User may upload or capture a personal photo (such as a wrist photo for watches or an upper-body/full-body photo for apparel) or a room photo (for furniture placement) to generate a synthesized preview image.

26.3.2. Every Virtual Try-On image is governed by the following mandatory notice: "Virtual try-on is an AI-generated preview and may not be 100% accurate. It is intended for illustration/preview purposes only and is not a guarantee of the actual product's appearance or fit."

26.3.3. By uploading or capturing a photo for Virtual Try-On or visual product search, the End User confirms that they have the legal right to submit the photo, that the photo does not contain unlawful or explicit material, and that the photo may be processed by automated vision safety filters and image-generation models solely to return the requested preview or search result.

### 26.4. End-User Session Storage, Data Retention, and Privacy Rights

26.4.1. For the purpose of maintaining conversation continuity during a shopping session, the AI Agent retains the End User's chat history, session state, and any contact details voluntarily shared in chat for twenty-four (24) hours (86,400 seconds) from the End User's last message, and stores a local copy in the End User's web browser storage for up to twenty-four (24) hours (which the End User may clear at any time using the reset chat button or browser settings), as detailed in [Annex C (annexes/annex-c-data-processing-and-retention.en.md)](./annex-c-data-processing-and-retention.en.md).

26.4.2. To help the Merchant understand visitor engagement and improve its store responses, the Platform records de-identified Visitor Analytics (such as session duration, message counts, general device/browser category, and unanswered product topics) keyed by a one-way cryptographic visitor hash for ninety (90) days.

26.4.3. The Merchant is the Personal Data Controller for the End User's store interactions and customer inquiries. End Users wishing to exercise their statutory data protection rights regarding their relationship with the Merchant should contact the Merchant directly using the contact channels provided on the Merchant's storefront.

### 26.5. Acceptable Conduct by End Users and Preservation of Mandatory Consumer Rights

26.5.1. End Users must not submit unlawful, abusive, or harmful content, attempt to manipulate or bypass the AI Agent's safety instructions (prompt injection), upload malicious files, or send automated flood requests exceeding the session rate limits configured for the AI Agent.

26.5.2. Nothing in this Annex F excludes, limits, or overrides any mandatory statutory consumer protection right or data protection right enjoyed by an End User under applicable law, including Law No. 8 of 1999 of the Republic of Indonesia on Consumer Protection and Law No. 27 of 2022 of the Republic of Indonesia on Personal Data Protection.
