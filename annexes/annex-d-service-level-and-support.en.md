### 24.1. Service Availability Target and Exclusions

24.1.1. Pursuant to [Article 14 of the Agreement (terms-and-conditions.en.md)](../terms-and-conditions.en.md#14-article-14-service-availability-maintenance-updates-and-customer-support), the Provider targets a monthly Platform availability of [UPTIME TARGET, e.g., 99.0%] for the Main Dashboard and deployed AI Agent runtime environments during each calendar month.

24.1.2. The availability calculation excludes unavailability or performance degradation resulting from:
(a) Scheduled Maintenance notified in accordance with Section 24.2.
(b) Emergency maintenance or security mitigation under [Section 14.2 of the Agreement](../terms-and-conditions.en.md#142-emergency-maintenance-security-patching-and-feature-updates).
(c) Outages, rate limits, interface changes, or webhook delays of WhatsApp, Telegram, or upstream third-party AI Model providers.
(d) Exhaustion of the Customer's Monthly Package Quota, Monthly Credit Pool, or Credit Balance, or triggering of Visitor Session Rate Limits configured by the Customer.
(e) Suspension of the Customer's subscription or AI Agent due to overdue payment ([Article 7 of the Agreement](../terms-and-conditions.en.md#7-article-7-overdue-payments-grace-period-suspension-cancellation-and-data-retention)) or Acceptable Use violation ([Article 8](../terms-and-conditions.en.md#8-article-8-customer-obligations-acceptable-use-and-prohibited-conduct) and [Article 18 of the Agreement](../terms-and-conditions.en.md#18-article-18-term-administrative-suspension-termination-and-survival), and [Annex B (annexes/annex-b-acceptable-use-and-uploads.en.md)](./annex-b-acceptable-use-and-uploads.en.md)).
(f) Faults in the Customer's own external website hosting the Web Widget, invalid Third-Party Channel tokens supplied by the Customer, or Force Majeure events under [Section 20.4 of the Agreement](../terms-and-conditions.en.md#204-force-majeure).

24.2.1. Routine Scheduled Maintenance is preferentially conducted during off-peak hours ([PREFERRED MAINTENANCE WINDOW, e.g., 00:00 to 05:00 Western Indonesia Time / WIB]) with at least [MAINTENANCE NOTICE HOURS, e.g., twenty-four (24) hours] advance notice via the Main Dashboard or email.

24.3.1. Customers may submit support inquiries through the following official channels during [SUPPORT OPERATING HOURS, e.g., Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 WIB, excluding Indonesian public holidays]:
(a) Email: [SUPPORT EMAIL]
(b) WhatsApp Support: [SUPPORT WHATSAPP NUMBER]
(c) Telegram Support: [SUPPORT TELEGRAM HANDLE]

(a) Critical Severity (complete outage of deployed AI Agents or payment settlement blockage): Within [CRITICAL RESPONSE HOURS, e.g., four (4) business hours].
